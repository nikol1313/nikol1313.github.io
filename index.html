₾ Price tracking

iPhone Price Tracker

Compares and tracks iPhone prices across Georgian stores.

The problem

The same iPhone costs different amounts at different Georgian retailers, and prices change often. Finding the best deal meant opening several stores and re-checking them by hand, again and again.

What I built
  • Retailer-specific scrapers pull listings from multiple Georgian stores, including Zoommer, Alta and more, into one searchable catalog and log every crawl.
  • Every price change is recorded, so each model gets its own price history.
  • Prices are re-checked every 12 hours, and a Telegram alert goes out when a tracked iPhone drops below the user’s target price.

▶ Media tools

Video Downloader

Paste a link, pick a quality or MP3, and watch it download live.

Video Downloader
The problem

Growing up, I downloaded songs to my device from the “YouTube to MP3” sites that come up on Google, but they were full of ads and sometimes viruses. So I built my own clean downloader that fits my preferences and runs long downloads in the background.

What I built
  • yt-dlp downloads videos from YouTube and other supported sites in 360p, 720p, or 1080p, or as audio-only MP3, and FFmpeg merges video and audio.
  • A Node.js, Express, and TypeScript backend runs yt-dlp as background processes, two at a time, while PostgreSQL stores every download job.
  • A one-page React and TypeScript interface starts downloads, shows live progress, and saves finished files.
  • Prometheus collects app metrics at /metrics, and Docker Compose runs the whole stack.

◎ In progress · AI automation

ModzebnAI

An AI job radar that ranks new Georgian job postings against your CV.

The problem

Job hunting in Georgia means checking jobs.ge, hr.ge and other sites every day, reading dozens of postings that don’t fit, and rewriting the same cover letter again and again.

What I’m building
  • Scheduled scrapers collect new postings from Georgian job boards into one PostgreSQL database.
  • AI compares each posting with your CV, scores how well it fits, and explains why.
  • Strong matches trigger an email notification, along with an AI-drafted cover letter tailored to the job.
  • A React and TypeScript dashboard to browse matches, fit scores, and generated cover letters.
